# Saving protection changes

File browser selections are pending edits until **Save selections** succeeds. Saving them preserves the vault's other settings, including paused protection, PostgreSQL, maintenance, synchronisation and diagnostics settings. A saved selection is not a completed backup. Removing a selection and saving stops protection while keeping its existing history, subject to the saved retention policy. Saving never asks for or performs history deletion.

**Pause protection** stops new captures, including manual backup. An already accepted capture finishes before Pause is acknowledged. **Resume protection** allows captures using the saved selections. Both actions keep independent unsaved edits and preserve history and recovery. This NEXT-004 release does not yet provide automatic capture.

When protection is enabled, **Run backup** first saves the current draft. It requests backup once, for that same vault and acknowledged revision, only after the service confirms success. A rejected, cancelled or unconfirmed save keeps the edits and explains why this action's backup did not start.

The save result stays beside the pending changes through refresh and Explorer **Show versions**. Retry saves the retained draft. **Discard changes** reloads saved settings; if reload fails or newer edits arrive, the draft stays visible and the app explains that discard did not complete.

A lost acknowledgement may mean the service already committed the operation. The app retains its original identity and provides **Check original outcome**; it never blindly replays an uncertain save or control. Local editing drafts survive a desktop restart independently of submitted operations. Historical combined save/purge records remain readable and their original receipts remain resolvable. New combined requests are refused.

Edits made during a save remain pending against the newly acknowledged baseline. They must be saved before backup. Both Options entry points require loaded settings and a saved or discarded draft. Options remains open until an in-flight save finishes. Protection saving stays unavailable after closing Options until saved settings reload successfully. If newer selection edits arrive during that reload, they stay pending; review them before explicitly discarding to reload. Explorer protection requests received while Options is open or reconciliation is incomplete explain that they must be retried afterwards.

**Review history deletion…** in Repository reviews all versions of the selected file, or the selected folder and its descendants. It shows the exact scope, candidates, logical byte count and retained dependencies. The complete review is bounded by the existing 256-item browse limit; choose a smaller scope when the review is incomplete. Preview requires history, maintenance and deletion authority; execution requires maintenance and deletion authority.

Deletion is unavailable while the scope overlaps saved protection, even when protection is paused. In this bounded release, regex exclusions within a saved protected root do not authorise history deletion; remove and save the overlapping protection root first. Outside-scope lineage or folder references prevent deletion even when those dependent versions are unprotected. The service derives protection from its own accepted configuration and checks the full manifest graph under the repository lease. A changed configuration or history requires another review.

**Delete reviewed history…** asks separately for confirmation of that exact scope and count. Cancellation has no effects. Confirmed deletion removes reviewed repository/mirror history and unreferenced chunks, keeping live source files and unrelated recoverable history. Software rollback cannot undo deliberate history deletion.

An uncertain or partial deletion keeps its original operation across desktop restart. **Check original deletion outcome** queries that receipt without re-executing deletion. The desktop blocks changes to protection or repository state while that record remains unresolved; history, recovery and diagnostics remain available. The service also refuses fresh destructive requests from other clients while any deletion remains admitted and unresolved. An admitted operation with no completed receipt stays uncertain; a timeout is not proof of success or failure and does not authorise retry or rollback to an older service.

The [NEXT-004 verification record](verification/2026-10-08-next004-controls/README.md) distinguishes component, native and installed acceptance. The accepted [save regression evidence](verification/2026-10-03-protection-save/README.md) remains part of the baseline.
